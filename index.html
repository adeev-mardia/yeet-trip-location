Getting there

How the travel works

Flights from Chennai are around ₹25,000–30,000 return, depending on dates. That could go up or down closer to booking. We stay on Havelock (Swaraj Dweep), which is a ferry ride from Port Blair. The ferry is about ₹4,000 return.

  1. 1
    Day 1: Chennai to Port BlairMorning flight, landing around 11am–12pm.
  2. 2
    Day 1: Straight onto the ferry to HavelockNo stop in Port Blair. Check into the Havelock hotel the same day.
  3. 3
    Days on HavelockDiving course, beaches, activities.
  4. 4
    Second-last day: afternoon ferry back to Port BlairCheck into a Port Blair hotel for one night.
  5. 5
    Last day: morning flight to Chennai
Why the extra night in Port Blair? Flights out of Port Blair leave in the morning, but ferries from Havelock don't get back to Port Blair in time to catch them. So we come back the afternoon before and sleep in Port Blair.
No flying for 24 hours after diving.The last dive has to be at least a full day before the flight home. The Port Blair night helps with this, but plan the course days so the last dive isn't on the final Havelock day.
Diving

Diving with Dive India

I'm going with Dive India on Havelock. It's one of the best-known dive centres in the Andamans and is internationally certified with SSI (Scuba Schools International). Diving is not the thing to save money on: a cheap operator can mean worn-out gear and rushed instructors. We're all doing the same course.

Our pick

Intro to Scuba

₹14,000 / person · 2 days
  • A guided first taste of diving with an instructor
  • Shallow dives only
  • No certification at the end
Beginner certificate

Scuba course

₹27,000 / person · 3 days
  • SSI's entry-level certification (Scuba Diver)
  • You can dive again later, but only with a dive professional
  • Shallower depth limit, typically 12 m
  • Can be upgraded to Open Water later
Full certificate

Open Water

₹36,000 / person · 4 days
  • The full SSI Open Water Diver certification, recognised worldwide
  • Dive anywhere with a buddy, no instructor needed
  • Certified to 18 m
  • Includes gear, study material, at least 4 training dives, snacks on dive days and taxes
Scuba course vs Open Water, simply: both give you a certificate. The 3-day course is a supervised licence: you can only dive with a pro and not as deep. Open Water is a full licence: you and a buddy can dive on your own, deeper, anywhere in the world. If we might dive again on future trips, Open Water is worth the extra ₹9,000.
